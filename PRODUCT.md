# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (Bootstrap 4, custom SCSS/CSS, jQuery, owl.carousel). No build tooling or framework. Single-page site (index.html) with anchor-linked sections.

## Users

Two audiences, served evenly:
- Recruiters and hiring managers actively evaluating Hemaksh for full-time roles (software engineering, production/reliability engineering, ML, quant).
- Broader professional network (LinkedIn connections, conference/referral contacts, collaborators) encountering the site outside an active hiring process.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio for Hemaksh Chaturvedi. Presents work history, projects, education, and skills, and provides a path to resume download and direct contact. Success means a visitor quickly understands his range and depth and can act (download resume, reach out, view code).

## Positioning

Proves both range and depth in a way a resume's dense bullet format can't: range across domains (quant development, ML, full-stack, production/reliability engineering), plus depth of technical knowledge in each, shown through named projects, real metrics, and concrete systems worked on — not just a list of skills.

## Operating Context

Sections: About/hero, Projects, Experience (resume/timeline), Education, Contact. Nav includes a resume PDF download link (resume/Hemaksh-Chaturvedi.pdf). Existing projects and work-history entries currently on the site are being kept and extended, not replaced.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- No backend; content is static HTML.
- Resume PDF is a linked, downloadable asset — must stay in sync with on-page experience content.
- Existing project list and prior work-history entries (SovAI, NYU Grad Assistant, Fellowship.AI, ISPA EXIM, Sortizy, and the 6 listed projects) are to remain; new roles (Deloitte, MTA) and a skills section are additions, not a replacement of what's there.

## Evidence on Hand

- Resume: resume/Hemaksh-Chaturvedi.pdf (source of truth for current role titles, dates, and metrics).
- Existing project links: GitHub repos per project (see index.html project cards) and one external NYU newsletter link.
- Contact: chaturvedihemaksh@gmail.com, GitHub/Hemaksh14, LinkedIn/hemaksh-chaturvedi.

## Product Principles

- Additive by default: extend the timeline and skills rather than removing existing projects/history unless explicitly asked.
- Resume PDF and on-page content should stay consistent with each other.
- Show range (quant/ML/full-stack/production) and depth (real systems, real metrics) together — neither alone is the goal.
